# Ardiles SS27 reference

A topic-based Indonesian reading guide adapted from the approved Ardiles Launching SS 2027 presentation.

Live site: https://ilham-rizaldi.github.io/ardiles-ss27-reference/

## Contents

1. Brand direction
2. Lifestyle
3. Performance
4. Running product development
5. Research and testing
6. Football and futsal
7. Sales channels
8. Distribution and growth

The guide combines repeated slide content, corrects spelling and capitalization, and presents related information in readable paragraphs and tables. It includes topic navigation, full-text topic search, a print/PDF button, keyboard focus styles and responsive layouts. Open `index.html` directly; there is no build step or external dependency.

## Source and limits

Source: https://canva.link/49b1pgjrrpu7fvx

Text was reread on 1 November 2026 and matched the extraction used for this revision. The source presentation was described as approved by the user. The edited guide is a new editorial adaptation, not an independent validation of product or business claims.

Twenty-one source slides have no extractable text: 21, 68, 112, 123–124 and 134–149. Their visual content has not been reconstructed. Template filler on slides 166–168 and decorative page numbers are excluded. Numerical and terminology inconsistencies are noted in their relevant topics. Source-page ranges are retained as references, rather than as the site's reading structure.
